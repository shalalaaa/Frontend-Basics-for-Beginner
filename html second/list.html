Manga, Manhua, Manhwa

We would understand diffrence between all three
Manga
Japanese comics or graphic novels, usually read from right to left. They are traditionally published in black and white.
Manhua
Chinese comics or graphic novels, usually read from left to right. They can be in color or black and white, and many are published as digital webcomics.
Manhwa
Korean comics or graphic novels, usually read from left to right. Many modern manhwa are full-color webcomics read vertically.
