Louis Tomlinson Discography

Louis Tomlinson is a very talented English singer and songwriter born on December 24, 1991, in Doncaster, England. He began his music career as a member of the famous boy band One Direction, which was formed on The X Factor UK in 2010. The band consisted of five members: Louis Tomlinson, Harry Styles, Niall Horan, Liam Payne, and Zayn Malik. Although they competed as solo contestants, the judges decided to bring them together to form a band. One Direction became one of the most successful boy bands in the world, gaining millions of fans through hit songs such as "What Makes You Beautiful," "Story of My Life," and "Night Changes."
After the band went on hiatus in 2016, Louis began his solo career and developed his own musical style. He released his debut solo album, Walls, in 2020, followed by Faith in the Future in 2022.
